Android Auto Maker On this computer

Show an app on Android Auto while parked

Upload an APK or XAPK that was not built for the car. This tool decodes it, declares it as a game, adds the Android Auto launcher, rebuilds it, and signs a file you can install.

APK or XAPK

What changes in the manifest

  1. Declared as a game. android:appCategory="game" tells Android Auto this app may show its own screen while the car is parked.
  2. Added to the car launcher. The main activity gets android.intent.category.CAR_LAUNCHER, which is how Android Auto opens it.
  3. Still installable on a phone. Car hardware is declared optional. Every APK in an XAPK is then signed with the same new key and packed back into an XAPK.

Android Auto shows the app while the car is parked and hides it while the car is moving. The phone needs Android 15 or newer. Flags that would mark the app as safe to use while driving are removed, so the car keeps it in the parked category.
